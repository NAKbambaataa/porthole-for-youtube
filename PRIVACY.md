# Privacy Policy: Porthole for YouTube

_Last updated: 2026-11-13_

**Porthole for YouTube does not collect, transmit, sell, or share any personal data.**

## What the extension does
Porthole adds a button to YouTube watch pages that moves the video you're watching into a floating, always-on-top window, and moves it back when you close that window.

## Data we collect
None. Porthole has no servers, no accounts, no analytics, no telemetry, no advertising, and no third-party code.

## Data stored on your device
Porthole saves your preferences (for example your keyboard shortcut, starting window width, and whether captions are mirrored) using Chrome's `storage` API. If you are signed in to Chrome with sync turned on, Chrome may sync these preferences between your own devices as part of its normal extension-settings sync. We never receive this data.

## What the extension can see
Porthole runs only on `https://www.youtube.com/`. On those pages it reads the video element, the page title, and YouTube's caption text, solely to display them in the floating window on your own screen. This information is never stored, logged, or sent anywhere.

## Permissions
- **storage**: to save your preferences.
- **Access to www.youtube.com**: so the extension can add its button and move the video player into the floating window.

## Remote code
Porthole doesn't load or run any remote code. Everything it runs is bundled in the extension package.

## Children
Porthole doesn't knowingly collect information from anyone, including children.
