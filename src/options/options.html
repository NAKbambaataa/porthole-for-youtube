Porthole for YouTube

Settings sync across your signed-in Chrome browsers.

Pop-out

Keyboard shortcut Pops the video out or back in. Needs Alt, Ctrl or ⌘ so it never fires while typing.

Floating window

Shortcuts in the floating window

  • Space / K Play or pause
  • ← → / J L Skip back or forward
  • ↑ ↓ Volume
  • M Mute
  • < > Playback speed
  • Shift+N / Shift+P Next or previous video

Porthole is an independent project. It is not affiliated with, endorsed by, or sponsored by YouTube or Google. It collects no data.
